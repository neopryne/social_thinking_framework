Who is Athena?  Who is Abadar?  What do these people care about and what do they do?

This proposes a model of interacting with the world where smaller things try to create gods (by working under them), and the gods flicker in and out of existence and change as their supporters do.  Of course, such entities exist in a very real way [see the ways in which timeless beings exist], and once incorporated enough, may start to act in ways that surprise the individuals.

I'm not saying that these made up ideals will start doing miracles, they probably* will not.  I'm still not sure how reality fundamentally works.  The aspect that we as the things they run on care about is that by using these constructs, we can align ourselves with each other better, and better understand our relations.
